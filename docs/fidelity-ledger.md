# Design fidelity ledger

Comparison reference: `docs/design-concepts/hero-concept.png`. Implementation evidence: `artifacts/screenshots/playwright-desktop-hero.png` and the viewport matrix in `artifacts/screenshots/`.

## Matched

- Graphite cinematic material macro with controlled warm highlights.
- Large condensed uppercase editorial headline at the left, small red eyebrow, rectangular red CTA and restrained secondary action.
- Fixed official brand header, right-side numbered chapter rail, bottom scroll cue.
- Red/graphite/porcelain system, sparse glass treatment and strong industrial spacing.
- Dense proof transition immediately after the long-form HERO story.

## Intentional changes

- The generated concept’s approximate wordmark was replaced by the official SVG from the current Lemark site.
- Generated concept copy was replaced by verified Russian production copy from the master prompt/current site.
- A decorative preview strip shown in the concept was replaced by a functional seven-chapter progress rail and the following proof bar.
- HERO background is a 36.375 s bidirectional scroll-scrub video assembled from nine generated shots, not a static concept image.
- Mobile uses a vertical delivery encode, narrower typography and shorter visible descriptions while preserving chapter order and CTA access.

## QA conclusion

The implementation preserves the concept’s composition, hierarchy, palette and industrial tone while overriding unverified/generated brand details with official assets and verified content. Functional deviations directly support the sticky scroll-story requirement.
